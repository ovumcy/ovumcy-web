### Fixed

- **Long notes and symptom names stay inside the calendar day panel.** An unbroken note (a long
  link, say) or a 40-character custom symptom name widened the whole calendar page on a phone,
  month grid included, to one weekday per screen. Both now wrap inside the panel.
- **Calendar navigation details.** The day panel's heading names the year for a day outside the
  current one; Previous, Next and Today keep the selected day open; saving a day refreshes the
  month once instead of twice; a phone marks today in the grid again; and a link that opens a day
  for editing (the dashboard's yesterday and missed-day links) brings the panel into view on a
  phone.
