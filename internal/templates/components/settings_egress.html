They were two, and a heading state that is an automaton over BOTH — "something here needs attention" outranks "something here is enabled" — cannot be assembled from two cards that each only know their own half. This card replaces ITSELF through htmx (hx-swap="outerHTML"), so every control below targets #settings-egress and the server re-renders the whole thing from a read taken after the write. A response that swapped only the status line would leave a state sentence the write had just falsified standing beside a fresh success message. Replacing itself is also why the card declares data-status-island-host: the status machinery is handed the card, not the island inside it, and resolves the island from the nearest ancestor declaring that it hosts one. Without it a save and an error both complete and the page says nothing. It is NOT data-success-toast — that one is an island's opt-in to a toast, and this section answers in place rather than over the page. The medical-safety disclaimer is rendered ONCE here, for the merged section, and stays in the body beside the configuration it qualifies: a closed card hides the two together. */}}
